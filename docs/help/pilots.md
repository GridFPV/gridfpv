# Pilots and classes

These two lists belong to Grid, not to one event. Add a pilot once and they are there for
every event.

## Pilots

**Add pilot** needs only a callsign. The rest is optional: real name, team, video system,
colour, country, and ids from other systems. **Phonetic** is how the callsign is said out
loud. Lap callouts on the Run page use it.

- **Edit** changes a pilot. Their past results follow the pilot, not the spelling.
- **Remove** takes a pilot out of the list. Races they already flew keep their laps.

A pilot is put into a race in one of two ways. A heat can name them directly on the
[Phases](#/help/phases) page. Or they are put on a class's roster on the
[Details](#/help/event) page, and a heat is filled from that class.

## Pilots and your server

If the open event is sent to a club on a [server](#/help/server), a pilot can be tied to
their account there. The pilot then sees their own race results when they sign in to the server.

- **Account**, beside a pilot, asks **Who is this on the server?** Pick one of your club's
  members by name, or **Nobody**. The pilot's row then says whose account it is.
- **Add from the server** brings pilots in that the server has and Grid does not. They
  come in already tied to their accounts. It also offers members who have no pilot yet,
  and makes a pilot for each one you pick.

Both ask the club of the open event when you press them, so the server must be in reach.
Racing does not need it.

A pilot can also get tied on the server's own page, with an invite made for them or by
asking the club. Grid picks that up by itself in a few seconds.

Only a callsign and a colour are sent to the server. Real names stay on your computer.

## Classes

The **Classes** page, also under Setup, holds the classes an event can race. The standard FPV classes
come with Grid. **Add class** makes one of your own; it needs only a name.

**Org** is whose class it is, such as MultiGP or your own club. It shows as a small tag
beside the class. Type anything up to 24 letters, or leave it empty for no tag.

The classes that come with Grid are like any you make. Each one has:

- **Edit** to change its name, org, reference or description.
- **Hide** to keep it in this list and take it out of the list an event picks from.
- **Remove** to take it away.

If you remove a class that came with Grid and change your mind, **Put them back**, at the
bottom of the page, brings back every one you removed, as it was when Grid was new.

A class in this list is only a name. Which classes an event races, and who is in each, is
set on the [Details](#/help/event) page.

## Teams

Grid has no teams yet. Every race is between pilots.
