# Pilots and classes

These two lists belong to Grid, not to one event. Add a pilot once and they are there for
every event.

## Pilots

**Add pilot** needs only a callsign. The rest is optional: real name, team, video system,
colour, country, and ids from other systems. **Phonetic** is how the callsign is said out
loud. Lap callouts on the Run page use it.

- **Edit** changes a pilot. Their past results follow the pilot, not the spelling.
- **Remove** takes a pilot out of the list. Races they already flew keep their laps.

A pilot is put into a race in one of two ways. A heat can name them directly on the
[Phases](#/help/phases) page. Or they are put on a class's roster on the
[Details](#/help/event) page, and a heat is filled from that class.

## Classes

The **Classes** page, also under Setup, holds the classes an event can race. The standard FPV classes
come with Grid. **Add class** makes one of your own; it needs only a name.

**Org** is whose class it is, such as MultiGP or your own club. It shows as a small tag
beside the class. Type anything up to 24 letters, or leave it empty for no tag.

A built-in class cannot be changed, but you do not have to keep it:

- **Hide** keeps it in this list and takes it out of the list an event picks from.
- **Remove** takes it away. If you change your mind, **Put the built-in classes back**, at
  the bottom of the page, brings back every one you removed.

A class in this list is only a name. Which classes an event races, and who is in each, is
set on the [Details](#/help/event) page.

## Teams

Grid has no teams yet. Every race is between pilots.
