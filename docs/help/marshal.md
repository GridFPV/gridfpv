# Marshal

Look at a race again and fix it. Every change here is a **ruling**: a decision you make,
which Grid writes down. A ruling can be undone.

## Picking a race

The page opens on the race that most needs you: the first on the to-do list, or else the
newest race.

- **To do** lists races flagged **Needs a look**, races with an open protest, and stopped
  races not yet saved or discarded. Each says why it is there.
- **Races** lists the other races, newest first. It shows one phase at a time, starting
  with the one being raced. The box above it picks another phase or all of them.
- **Show discarded races** adds the races that were thrown away.

## One seat at a time

You marshal one pilot at a time. Under the races is **Seats**, with a button for each seat
in the race. It shows the pilot, the seat, how many laps they have, and how many passes
do not count. Click one to see that pilot's laps. Your pick stays when you open another
race.

The video is the same for every seat. It is one recording of the whole race.

## The trace

The seat has a picture of its signal for the whole race. The solid line is the enter
level and the dashed line is the exit level. Marks along the top show the start, the hole
shot, and each lap.

- **+** and **−** zoom. The arrows move left and right. **Whole race** fits it all.
- Click a lap's name in the table to find it on the trace.
- Click an empty spot on the trace to add a lap there.
- Drag a lap's mark to move it. A finger works too: slide the trace, or drag a mark.

## The video

If the race was recorded, a **Video** card shows above the seats. It plays the race's
video, lined up with the race clock. There is nothing to line up by hand: Grid knows when
the recording started and when the race started.

- It plays the video **with the overlay** when there is one. The clock drawn on it is the
  thing to read a pass against. If laps have changed since it was made, it says **Laps
  changed since**. The laps on it are old, but its clock is still right.
- **Clean, no overlay** plays the plain recording.
- The line on each signal trace is where the video is. It moves as the video plays.
- **Click a trace** to jump the video to that moment. Clicking a lap in the table does the
  same.
- **◀ Frame** and **Frame ▶** step one frame. **0.25×** and **0.5×** play slowly.
- **To the start** goes to the start tone.
- The **space bar** plays and pauses from anywhere on the page. In a text box it types a
  space as usual.
- **Add a lap** is always at the moment the video is on. Play, step or click to the pass,
  then press **Add a lap here**. If you type a time in the box, the video goes there.
- **Hide video** puts it away. Grid remembers your choice.

A race with no recording has no Video card. If the file was moved or cannot be played, the
card says so and names the file. MP4 always plays.

A seat on a timer whose clock was never measured cannot be lined up, so its trace shows no
line.

## Fixing laps

- **Remove** takes a pass out. Its time joins the next lap. Say laps A, B and C were flown
  and B was a false reading. Remove B and lap C now runs from the end of A.
- **Mark as restart** is for a pilot who crashed and started again. That lap gets no time,
  and the laps either side stay as they were. A "fastest laps in a row" run never crosses a
  restart.
- **Add a lap** puts in a pass the gate missed. It is right under the trace. Type the
  seconds into the race.
  If the gate missed the **hole shot**, add it the same way. A pass you add before the
  first one becomes the hole shot, and the old first pass becomes the end of lap 1.
- **Move** a pass that was read at the wrong moment. Drag its mark along the trace, or
  click the lap, go to the right moment in the video, and press **Move here**. Grid shows
  the new lap times first. Nothing changes until you press **Move it**.
- **Crossings that do not count** lists passes that were left out, each with the reason.
  One that was too soon after the pass before it, or too soon after the start, has
  **Count it**. Press it if the lap was real. After that no lap rule leaves it out.
- A pass you removed is in the same list, with **Restore**.

The ranking under the seats is worked out again after each change.

## False start

**False start** on a seat records it against that pilot. Then discard the race on the Run
page and fly it again. A race never stands with a false start in it.

## Needs a look

**Looked at** clears the flag. **Needs a look** sets it on a race that was saved without
one.

## Protests

A protest is filed against one race. Pick who it is by, type what it is about, and press
**File protest**. Settle it with **Upheld**, **Denied** or **Withdrawn**. A phase cannot be
locked while it holds an open protest.

## Locked races

A race under a locked phase is official. It is shown but cannot be changed. Unlock the
phase on the [Phases](#/help/phases) page first.
