# Marshal

Look at a race again and fix it. Every change here is a **ruling**: a decision you make,
which Grid writes down. A ruling can be undone.

## Picking a race

The page opens on the race that most needs you: the first on the to-do list, or else the
newest race.

- **To do** lists races flagged **Needs a look**, races with an open protest, and stopped
  races not yet saved or discarded. Each says why it is there.
- **Races** lists the other races, newest first. It shows one phase at a time, starting
  with the one being raced. The box above it picks another phase or all of them.
- **Show discarded races** adds the races that were thrown away.

## The trace

Each seat has a picture of its signal for the whole race. The solid line is the enter
level and the dashed line is the exit level. Marks along the top show the start, the hole
shot, and each lap.

- **+** and **−** zoom. The arrows move left and right. **Whole race** fits it all.
- Click a lap's name in the table to find it on the trace.
- Click an empty spot on the trace to add a lap there.

## Fixing laps

- **Remove** takes a pass out. Its time joins the next lap. Say laps A, B and C were flown
  and B was a false reading. Remove B and lap C now runs from the end of A.
- **Mark as restart** is for a pilot who crashed and started again. That lap gets no time,
  and the laps either side stay as they were. A "fastest laps in a row" run never crosses a
  restart.
- **Add a lap** puts in a pass the gate missed. Type the seconds into the race.
- **Crossings that do not count** lists passes that were left out, each with the reason.
  One that was too soon after the pass before it, or too soon after the start, has
  **Count it**. Press it if the lap was real. After that no lap rule leaves it out.
- A pass you removed is in the same list, with **Restore**.

The ranking under the seats is worked out again after each change.

## False start

**False start** on a seat records it against that pilot. Then discard the race on the Run
page and fly it again. A race never stands with a false start in it.

## Needs a look

**Looked at** clears the flag. **Needs a look** sets it on a race that was saved without
one.

## Protests

A protest is filed against one race. Pick who it is by, type what it is about, and press
**File protest**. Settle it with **Upheld**, **Denied** or **Withdrawn**. A phase cannot be
locked while it holds an open protest.

## Locked races

A race under a locked phase is official. It is shown but cannot be changed. Unlock the
phase on the [Phases](#/help/phases) page first.
