# Run

Race day in one screen. Put a heat on the timer, start it, stop it, then save or discard.

## On the timer

Two boxes pick the heat. The first picks the **phase**. The second picks a **heat** in that
phase. A heat's name shows where it stands: how many races it has, or that it is complete,
waiting or locked.

With no heat picked by you, the page shows the heat with a race in progress, or the first
heat of **Up next**. The boxes are locked while a race is counting down, running, or
waiting to be saved.

Under the boxes are the heat's format and the timer that times it.

## Start, Stop, Save, Discard

The big button follows the race.

1. **Start** begins the countdown, with three short beeps and a long start tone. A format
   with no countdown starts at once.
2. **Call off** cancels during the countdown.
3. **Stop** ends the race.
4. **Save** keeps the race. **Discard** throws it away. Nothing is saved by itself. A
   stopped race waits for you, and no other race can start until you choose.

**Save options** adds a **Needs a look** flag and a note. A flagged race goes on the
[Marshal](#/help/marshal) to-do list.

**Discard** asks for a reason: false start, crash, timer problem, or other. For a false
start, pick the pilot. A discarded race is kept in the event's history but never counts.

**Call heat done** ends a heat whose format lets you decide. **Advance without flying**
moves a pilot on when they are alone in a bracket heat.

## Keyboard

For running the night alone, the buttons have keys.

- **Space** does the next step: Start, then Stop, then Save. It never discards.
- **z** starts. **x** stops, or calls off a countdown.
- **s** or **c** saves.
- **d** pressed twice within three seconds discards. **v** works too. The Discard button
  says "d again" while it waits.
- **?** shows the list.

Keys do nothing while you type in a field or a dialog is open.

## Seat cards

One card for each seat in the heat. During a race it shows the pilot's place, lap count,
last lap and best lap, and every lap in order. The fastest lap has a green border.

In a heat ranked on the fastest laps in a row, the card also shows **Best 3** for this
race and **Heat best 3** for the whole heat. The laps that make up this race's best run
are filled in.

**tune** opens the seat's tuning page. A **Bench** seat has a **Crossing** button, which
logs a pass by hand.

## Sound

Your browser stays silent until you click something on the page. Pressing Start counts.

**Call out laps** speaks each new lap. With one pilot it says only the time. With more it
says the callsign, the lap number and the time. Untick it on a screen that should stay
quiet.

## Ranking and earlier races

**Ranking** is the heat's order so far. **Earlier races** lists each race that was saved or
discarded. **Fix the laps** opens a saved race on the Marshal page. **Discard** there throws
out a saved race, which is how a false start called late is handled.

## When Start is greyed out

The line above the buttons says why. The heat may be waiting on another heat, complete, or
locked. Or another heat has a race that is not saved or discarded yet, and a button takes
you to it.

A banner at the top says so if the heat's timer is not connected.
