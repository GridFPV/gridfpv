# Results

What the event has decided so far.

## Phase results

Pick a phase to see its ranking and the numbers behind each place. The page opens on the
phase raced last.

- **Best 3 in a row** shows in a phase ranked that way. It is the pilot's best run in any
  saved race under the phase. Under the time is the race it was flown in. Press it to open
  that race.
- **Best lap** and **Most laps** are always shown. The best lap names its race too.
- **Wins** and **Points** show when the format ranks by them.
- **Set by hand** marks a place the director set. **Did not fly** marks a pilot with no
  race. **Disqualified** marks a pilot who was removed.

## Class standings

Under the phase results.

Each class is listed from first to last, with the phase that placed each pilot. A class's
standings come from the ranked phases made for that class. Grid works out which, with the
latest one first. The first phase decides. A later one is only used for pilots the first
one did not place. To change the order, or to leave a phase out, open the class on the
[Details](#/help/event) page.

Until a phase counts for a class, its card says so and shows no table.

## The races of a phase

Below the ranking is every race under the phase, newest first. Each one shows who finished
where. A discarded race is listed too, greyed out. It does not count.

## One race

Press a race's name to open it. You see each pilot with their place, laps, best lap and
total time, and then every lap in order. `HS` is the hole shot. The laps of the best run
are marked, and the best lap is in bold. If the race has a video, its file is named.

The trail at the top leads back to the phase. **Open in Marshal** opens the race to fix it.

## Provisional and official

A result is **provisional** until its phase is locked. Lock a phase on the
[Phases](#/help/phases) page when you are sure. It then shows as **official**.
